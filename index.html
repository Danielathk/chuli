Welcome!

I am a Research Associate in the Department of Chemistry at Northwestern University, working with Prof. Edward H. Sargent.

I am a computational scientist working at the intersection of first-principles calculations, atomistic simulation, kinetic modeling, and machine learning. My work spans phase transitions, chemistry under extreme conditions, 2D materials, and materials design, from ice nucleation, nanoconfined water, and carbon reactions in supercritical fluids to phonons in twisted bilayer graphene and materials discovery for perovskite solar cells. My current focus is machine learning accelerated discovery of materials for stable, high-performance perovskite solar cells, with the design pipeline increasingly automated by agentic AI.

Before Northwestern, I was a Research Assistant Professor and postdoctoral fellow at HKUST, working with Prof. Ding Pan and Prof. Xuhui Huang, and received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from HKUST under the supervision of Prof. Zhigang Li.
